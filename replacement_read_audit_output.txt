7.  CAPTURE IS ONE LEVEL DEEP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    The 2026-10-21 repair binds the contact replacement to the
    shipped `gfet.quantum_capacitance` CAPTURED AT MANAGER ENTRY,
    and V5 confirms the conflict is gone.  The two captured
    controls say why, and the reason is weaker than it looks.

    A cell protects the NAME it holds.  It does not protect the
    names the held OBJECT reads at call time:

        gfet.quantum_capacitance  live reads: [('graphene_fet_model', '_thermal_rounding')]
            in the rebind set: NONE -- safe to capture
        gfet.carrier_density      live reads: [('graphene_fet_model', '_thermal_rounding'), ('graphene_fet_model', 'quantum_capacitance')]
            in the rebind set: [('graphene_fet_model', 'quantum_capacitance')]  <-- NOT safe to capture

    Both captures look identical at the call site:
    `make_contact_cq(shipped, live=False)` and
    `make_captured_control(gfet.carrier_density)` are the same
    construction.  One is immune and one is not, and which it is
    depends entirely on what the captured object reads.  So the
    repair of 2026-10-21 is CORRECT and its correctness is
    CONTINGENT: had the contact needed the shipped charge
    relation rather than the shipped capacitance, the same repair
    would have been written, would have looked right, and would
    have left the defect in place.

    This is the third member of the family, stated in the form
    the other two take:
        2026-10-14  a repair to one binding is not a repair to
                    the class.
        2026-10-17  a class can span two operating points, so it
                    must be partitioned.
        2026-10-21  a replacement that READS a rebound name is
                    neither stale nor unassigned.
        TODAY       a capture that fixes one name does not fix
                    the names the captured object reads.  The
                    safe criterion is the TRANSITIVE live-read
