    THE PHYSICS.  v_F is not a constant of nature: it is
    renormalised by substrate screening, measured at
    1.15 +/- 0.02 e6 m/s on SiC, 1.49 +/- 0.08 e6 on BN and
    2.49 +/- 0.30 e6 on quartz, against a fully screened ab-initio
    value of 0.85e6 (C. Hwang, D. A. Siegel, S.-K. Mo, W. Regan,
    A. Ismach, Y. Zhang, A. Zettl, A. Lanzara, Sci. Rep. 2, 590
    (2012), doi:10.1038/srep00590).  The
    committed 1.00e6 sits just above the bare value, while a
    90 nm SiO2 back gate (eps ~ 3.9) sits between the BN and
    quartz cases, i.e. near 1.5e6 -- which is exactly the x1.5
    this probe applies by default.  So the -63.5273 % it reports
    for the contact C_q is not a synthetic perturbation: it is
    the SiO2-screened Fermi velocity, and Chapter 4 quotes the
    contact C_q at a v_F its own substrate does not have.
    The channel numbers do not move at all -- not because the
    channel is insensitive to v_F, but because its v_F is frozen
    into two precomputed constants (V11b, V11c).  A v_F
    sensitivity study done by editing gfet.v_F would have
    reported channel insensitivity as a RESULT.
