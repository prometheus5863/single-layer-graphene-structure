SECTION 6.  The premise: which potential is V_ch?
==============================================================================
  Pasadas and Jimenez (IEEE TED 2016, arXiv:1605.08235) write the GFET
  channel current as I = -W*Q_tot*v with v = mu*F, F = -dV/dx, and state
  that V(x) is the QUASI-FERMI LEVEL.  Under that reading Eq. (4) is
  already the complete drift-diffusion current and this module's term is
  a double count.  Under the electrostatic reading -- which is how V_ch
  enters C_ox*(V_g - V_ch - V_dirac) in carrier_density() -- Eq. (4) is
  drift-only and the term is missing physics.  The two readings differ by
  exactly lambda*V_F, so the number below is BOTH the diffusion current
  AND the size of an ambiguity that was already in every committed I_d.

  at the saturated-peak bias, V_ds = 0.1 V:
    I_d, electrostatic reading (lambda = 1) : 9.05299599e-05 A
    I_d, quasi-Fermi reading   (lambda = 0) : 9.00779072e-05 A
    SPREAD                                  : +0.5018 %
  and over the whole V_g sweep the spread stays within 0.5053 % (largest |value|, at V_g = -1.2720 V), with OPPOSITE SIGNS on the two branches.

  This does NOT withdraw a committed number.  It bounds one.  The RF
  thread's conclusions are ratios between runs of one model, and G5b
  shows the binding verdict survives either reading.  What it does end
  is the claim that this repository's RF numbers are drift-only slices:
  they are slices of a model whose driving potential was never named,
  and the correct repair is to NAME IT, not to add a term.
  Recommended, and recorded as the new top RF item: declare V_ch the
  quasi-Fermi potential (which is what the series factor in
  carrier_density() is for), keep lambda = 0 as the production path, and
  keep this module as the bound on the choice.  lambda = 1 is NOT
  wired into any other module by this commit.
