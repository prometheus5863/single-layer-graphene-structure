SECTION 4.  V_F is a POTENTIAL, and the census had it UNADJUDICATED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  graphene_potential_census_audit.py, committed earlier today, left
  `V_F` UNADJUDICATED in three modules on the guess that it was a
  Fermi VELOCITY written with a V_ prefix.  In
  graphene_diffusion_current_model.py it is not: fermi_voltage(n)
  returns E_F/e in VOLTS, and it is precisely the quantity that
  converts between the two readings of V_ch.  In
  graphene_contact_doping_nonlinear_model.py the same name IS a
  velocity, 1.0e6 m/s.  One name, two quantities, two modules --
  which is the 2026-10-01 fault (a name that cannot drift is the
  whole requirement) in a third place.
