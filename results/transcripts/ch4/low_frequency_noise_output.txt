SECTION 3.  THE IMPORTED SIGN CONVENTION NOTCHES THE HOLE BRANCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  alpha_c*mu*C_ox = 6.90627e-02 1/V (bias-INDEPENDENT: oxide and mobility only)
  g_m/I_d crosses -alpha_c*mu*C_ox at Vg = -2.900 V (g_m/I_d = -6.89049e-02 1/V)
  at that bias the two forms disagree by a factor 7.646e+05:
    verbatim signed form   : 6.1721e-13 1/Hz
    branch-corrected form  : 4.7194e-07 1/Hz
  its mirror bias Vg = +4.500 V (same |Vg-V_dirac|, electron side)
    both forms there       : 4.7850e-07 1/Hz (V8: bitwise equal)
  So the verbatim form makes two mirror-image bias points differ
  by 7.753e+05 x in trap-limited 1/f noise, with nothing physical
  distinguishing them.  Totals (CMF+Hooge), which is what a
  measurement would see: signed 6.3910e-08 vs corrected 5.3585e-07 1/Hz.
  The Hooge floor hides part of it -- the notch is a factor 8.384
  in the TOTAL, which is why it would be missed by eye on a log
  plot but not by a fit.  Checks V7/V7b/V8 pin all three claims.

SECTION 4.  THE M-SHAPE ONSET IS A TRAP-DENSITY THRESHOLD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  bisection on the PREDICATE dip>0 (dip_depth is identically 0
  over the whole Lambda region, so this is a region boundary and
  not a root), tol = 1e-4 decades, 17 iterations
  N_T* = 7.4737e+17 1/(eV cm^3)
  dip depth just above N_T*: +4.055e-08 decades
  dip depth just below N_T*: +0.000e+00 decades (exactly 0 => no dip)
  Below N_T* the predicted gate dependence is Lambda-shaped (the
  noise PEAKS at the charge-neutrality point); above it the CNF
  term wins on the flanks and opens the central dip, giving the
  M-shape the experimental literature reports.
  N_T* / 1e18 (top of the conventional Si/SiO2 interval) = 0.747
  N_T* / 1.1e20 (the fitted electrolyte-gated value)     = 0.00679
  => N_T* lies INSIDE the conventional 1e16..1e18 interval, at
     75% of its top end.
  SENSITIVITY TO THE HOOGE FLOOR.  N_T* is where CNF overtakes
  the Hooge term at the flanks, so it moves with alpha_H.  The
  three alpha_H values the same Table 1 lists for other devices:
    alpha_H = 3.0e-04  -> N_T* = 1.725e+17  (its Ref. 33, lowest)
    alpha_H = 1.3e-03  -> N_T* = 7.474e+17  (this model, L=13um row)
    alpha_H = 7.0e-03  -> N_T* = 4.024e+18  (its Ref. 32, highest)
  DOES THE SECTION-3 ARTEFACT MOVE THIS THRESHOLD?  Recomputing
  N_T* on the verbatim signed total instead of the corrected one:
    N_T*(branch-corrected) = 7.4737e+17
    N_T*(verbatim signed)  = 5.4660e+21   ratio 7313.6764
  The notch sits on one flank, and dip depth takes the MINIMUM of
  the two flank maxima, so the notched flank is the one that
  decides -- the artefact DOES move the threshold.
  Over that whole factor-23 span in alpha_H, N_T* stays below
  the fitted 1.1e20 and spans the top of the conventional
  interval rather than sitting far above it.

SECTION 5.  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DOES, AND IT IS THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT THIS MODULE WAS DRAFTED
  EXPECTING.  The draft prose asserted that the M-shape onset
  would sit far ABOVE the conventional SiO2 trap-density range,
  and therefore that an M-shape on a SiO2-gated GFET could not
  be homogeneous CNF.  The computed threshold refutes that:
  N_T* = 7.474e+17 1/(eV cm^3) is INSIDE the 1e16..1e18 interval.
  So on this device, homogeneous charge-number fluctuation at a
  perfectly ordinary oxide trap density already produces the
  M-shape, and the M-shape is NOT by itself evidence of the
  electron/hole-puddle inhomogeneity that Balandin's review
  attributes it to.  The shape discriminates mechanism only
  together with an independent trap-density measurement.
  DOES NOT: the puddle mechanism is not in this model at all --
  n_puddle here REGULARISES the density, it does not fluctuate.
  This result weakens the inference FROM shape TO mechanism; it
  says nothing about whether puddles also contribute.
  DOES NOT: contact noise (negligible near the CNP per
  arXiv:1806.06589, dominant at high |Vg|) is absent, so the
  flanks are a lower bound and the dip depth an upper one.
  DOES NOT: the CMF form and alpha_c are SUMMARY-ONLY provenance
  (that paper's supplement was not read verbatim).  V3/V7b pin
  the form's algebra, not the form.
  DOES NOT: N_T* is a threshold in THIS geometry at THIS mu and
  t_ox.  S_Vfb carries 1/(W L C_ox^2), so a thinner oxide or a
  larger area moves it; no scaling study is done here.
