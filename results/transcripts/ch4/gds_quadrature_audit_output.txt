PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS (recorded before measurement)
==========================================================================
  Q1: The shipped default dVds=1e-3 passes the 09-25 anchored step criterion (agreement at h/10 AND h/100 to rtol=1e-3) at the default operating point. Id(Vds) is close to linear at small Vds, so unlike H_DIFF the STEP is expected to be innocent.
  Q2: Nevertheless g_ds at the anchored step is biased by n_segments=50 at the >=0.1% level, and -- the discriminating half -- that bias is INVARIANT under step refinement: refining dVds by four decades changes it by less than 1%% of itself.
  Q3: The quadrature bias is FIRST order in 1/n (not second), because np.mean over an endpoint-inclusive linspace is not the trapezoid rule. Predicted: halving 1/(n-1) halves the residual.
  Q4: The `max(Vds - dVds, 1e-4)` guard is silently wrong whenever it fires, because it changes the interval but not the divisor 2*dVds. Predicted relative error >10%% at dVds=0.06, Vds=0.05.
  Q5: Propagated to f_max the bias is SMALLER than the bias in g_ds, because f_max ~ 1/sqrt(g_ds*(Rg+Rs) + 2*pi*f_T*Cgd*Rg): the square root halves it and the second denominator term dilutes it. Predicted f_max shift < half the g_ds shift.
  Q6: The 400-point Vg grid behind g_m (and hence behind the published peak f_T ~ 20 GHz) is a third entangled default. Predicted: peak f_T moves by <1%% under 16x Vg-grid refinement -- i.e. this one is innocent and the item can be closed rather than carried.
