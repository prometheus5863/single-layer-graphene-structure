Caveat: Ti, Cr, Cu, Ni, Au, Pd show *negative* R_extra (i.e. the junction region integrates to lower resistance than the
bulk channel alone). This is not a bug: when |n_contact| >> n_bulk
(a very strongly doped contact against a lightly gated bulk channel),
most of the 250 nm decay length is far better doped than the bulk
channel, so it locally conducts *better* than bulk over most of its
extent; only the narrow charge-neutrality crossing region is worse.
With the crossing spike now finite (puddle-regularized, see
_sheet_conductivity_puddle_regularized) rather than divergent, the
low-resistance well-doped stretch can dominate the net integral. This
is a real feature of the classical drift-sheet-conductance picture
used here, not a claim that the true device gets *easier* to contact
with such metals -- the model omits depletion-region/injection physics
right at the crossing itself, which would add resistance back in a way
this compact model cannot capture. See notes/2026-08-26-*.md, Section 4.

Result, corrected 2026-11-16 (doping measured from the n/p crossover W0 = 5.4 eV, not from graphene's 4.5 eV): every tabulated metal here has W < W0, so each contact n-dopes graphene, in the same sense as the n-type on-state channel, and more strongly. There is no p-n junction, the near-contact region conducts better than the bulk channel, and R_extra is NEGATIVE. The naive R_transmission is therefore positive for every metal and LARGER than the measured Rc. The claim is restricted to the physisorbed metals Cu, Au; Ni and Pd are chemisorbed and outside the work-function model, and their rows are printed only for continuity with the superseded table. The negative residuals reported before 2026-11-16 (Cu -787.5, Ni -360.5, Au -90.2 Ohm.um) were an artefact of measuring dWF from 4.5 eV, which p-doped these contacts against the n-type channel and created spurious p-n junctions. Caveat: the corrected model calls Au n-type at its tabulated polycrystalline W = 5.10 eV, while the DFT studies that place W0 at 5.4 eV find Au(111) p-type at their own W = 5.54 eV -- the W table and the crossover are not from the same source, and Au's sign is not established here.
