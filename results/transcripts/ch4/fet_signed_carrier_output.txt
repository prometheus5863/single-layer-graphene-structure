    and been silent about whether the gap was 2x or 200x.

  WHY THE SIGNED READING IS WHAT MAKES THIS DECIDABLE.
    n_puddle enters (D2) as a quadrature floor on a MAGNITUDE, and a
    magnitude cannot say whether the floor is on the net density,
    on the total, or on each species.  Those three readings differ
    by factors of 1, 1 and 2 in sigma_min, which is the same order
    as the disagreement itself -- so before Section 1 the gap was
    not quotable.  Section 1 settles it: the floored quantity is

    density the total would be 2*n_puddle and Chapter 4 would be
    4.1357 x too conductive instead of 2.0678 x -- the discrepancy
    moves the WRONG WAY under that reading, so the convention
    cannot be what explains it.

  WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED.  These two floors are not the same physics:
    4 q_e^2/h is a quantum/ballistic minimum conductivity observed
    experimentally, while e*mu*n_puddle is a diffusive disorder
    floor.  They are not required to be equal.  What IS a defect is
    that a thesis quotes both, in chapters that feed each other,
    without ever comparing them -- and that the diffusive one comes
    out BELOW the measured one, i.e. Chapter 4's channel is less
    resistive at neutrality than any measured graphene sheet.
