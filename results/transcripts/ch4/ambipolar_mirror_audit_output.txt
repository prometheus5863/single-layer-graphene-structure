SECTION 3.  THE FIGURE OF MERIT -- f_T's two centres
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C_gs is BITWISE even about V_dirac; |g_m| is even about
  V_dirac + Vds/2.  f_T = |g_m|/(2 pi C_gs) is therefore even
  about neither, and the two branch peaks of a device whose I_d
  is exactly symmetric come out UNEQUAL:

                              electron peak      hole peak      ratio e/h
  committed C_gs(Vch=0)       20.27790 GHz  20.27876 GHz   0.9999575857
  channel-averaged C_gs       20.27833 GHz  20.27833 GHz   1.0000000000
  peak bias, committed           2.810 V      -1.110 V

  Magnitude, honestly: at the PEAK the two capacitances barely
  differ, because there C_gs is C_ox-dominated and flat -- the
  peak-f_T asymmetry is 4.241e-05, i.e. the committed peak numbers in
  Chapter 4 are NOT materially wrong and none is withdrawn.  The
  mismatch is large where C_q matters, i.e. near neutrality:
    |Vg - V_m| = 0.05 V : committed C_gs differs between the two branches by  2.8472 %
    |Vg - V_m| = 0.10 V : committed C_gs differs between the two branches by  1.7404 %
    |Vg - V_m| = 0.20 V : committed C_gs differs between the two branches by  0.4297 %
    |Vg - V_m| = 0.50 V : committed C_gs differs between the two branches by  0.0656 %
    |Vg - V_m| = 1.00 V : committed C_gs differs between the two branches by  0.0163 %

  The claim is therefore about the SHAPE of f_T near the Dirac
  point, which is the region the repository's own RF chapter
  says it cannot be asked about for a different reason (the
  puddle floor, graphene_diffusion_current_model Section 3).

SECTION 6.  WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  DOES NOT: the channel-averaged C_gs is a MIDPOINT average of
  the source-end formula, not a derived non-reciprocal
  capacitance.  Jimenez arXiv:1105.2734 obtains C_gs by
  differentiating channel-integrated charges with Ward-Dutton
  partitioning; that is a different and better object, and it is
  not built here.  What is claimed is only that the averaged form
  shares a reflection centre with g_m and the source-end one
  does not.
  DOES NOT: `rf.gate_capacitance` is left UNCHANGED, so every
  committed f_T/f_max number in this repository is reproducible
  bit for bit.  Nothing in Chapter 4 is withdrawn by this module.
  DOES NOT: the law V_m = V_dirac + Vds/2 is a property of THIS
  model's symmetric channel-potential grid and of mu_e = mu_h.
  graphene_branch_asymmetric_mobility.py breaks the second
  premise on purpose; the centre is not expected to survive it,
  and that case is not measured here.
  DOES NOT: velocity saturation and diffusion (vsat, dd modules)
  integrate over the same profile but with an asymmetric
  velocity field; whether the centre survives there is OPEN.
