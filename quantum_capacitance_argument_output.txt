SECTION 2.  THE OBVIOUS FIX DELETES THE TEMPERATURE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  C_Q(n) = 2e*sqrt(n)/A_F is a T = 0 expression.  The committed
  function is thermally rounded for a stated reason -- its own
  docstring: "so C_q does not diverge to zero exactly at the
  neutrality point".  Substituting the dispersion removes that.

   C_q at the Dirac point                        F/m^2
   committed thermal, at dV = 0         8.43739892e-03
   T = 0 dispersion, at n = 0           0.00000000e+00
   thermal at E_F/e, at n = 0           8.43739892e-03
  [PASS] C_q at the Dirac point has a CLOSED FORM, PREFACTOR*(kT/e)*ln 4, and the thermal-at-E_F/e form reproduces it to the last bit while the dispersion gives exactly zero
         ln(2(1+cosh 0)) = ln 4 exactly, so C_q(0) = 8.4373989182e-03 F/m^2; the thermal-at-E_F/e form gives it to 0.0 ulp.  The dispersion gives 0, hence a series factor of 0 and an induced charge of EXACTLY 0 at the Dirac point -- the puddle floor would then be supplying 100 % of the density there rather than its committed 99.9 %.  The 2026-10-10 sizing of item 16 used that substitution, so its number is the cost of a fix that is wrong in a second way.

  A sharper statement of the same thing.  With the dispersion,
  n == 0 is a solution of dn/d(dV) = (C_ox/e)*f(C_Q(n)) through
  the origin, because f(C_Q(0)) = 0: the initial-value problem is
  degenerate there.  With the thermal form it is not.  Temperature
  is what makes the charge relation well-posed at the Dirac point,
  and that is the physics the obvious fix throws away.
