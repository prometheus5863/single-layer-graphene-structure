T4 -- acoustic-phonon mobility exponent: the 3D result in a 2D material
==============================================================================
  [PASS] T4a  mu_acoustic ~ 1/T, as graphene requires
        shipped exponent p in mu_ac ~ T^-p : 1.0000
        graphene requires p = 1, not 1.5.
        For longitudinal acoustic phonons the graphene resistivity is
          rho_LA = pi D_A^2 kB T / (4 e^2 hbar rho_s v_s^2 v_F^2),
        LINEAR in T and independent of carrier density, so at fixed n the
        mobility goes as 1/T. The T^-3/2 the code uses is the
        three-dimensional deformation-potential result, and the module's
        own comment states it as 'mu ~ T^(-1.5) at high T'.
        Hwang & Das Sarma, Phys. Rev. B 77, 115449 (2008).
        At 500 K this understates the mobility by a factor 1.000.
