Literature context (notes/2026-08-22-*.md, notes/2026-08-26-*.md):
  - Record intrinsic fT: ~300-427 GHz (exotic short/scaled devices)
  - Record fmax (60 nm T-gate, de-embedded): ~200 GHz
  - Feijoo et al. 2016 (200 nm gate, de-embedded): fT/fmax = 
    35.4/50 GHz (fmax/fT = 1.41); raw (pre-de-embedding): 21.3/42 GHz
  - Foundry-style 0.5-2 um gates, extrinsic: fT ~34 GHz, fmax ~37 GHz;
    extrapolated to Lg=50nm: ~100 GHz
  - This 200 nm-channel analytic estimate falls in the expected
    intermediate range between those regimes, which is a reasonable
    sanity check for a compact analytic (non-TCAD) model.

NOTE (revised 2026-08-26 -- see notes/2026-08-26-*.md, Section 1):
  f_max > f_T is NOT inherently unphysical for graphene FETs -- the
  earlier (2026-08-22) caveat treating it as a red flag was too broad.
  Feijoo et al. 2016 report f_max > f_T at every gate length they
  measured (low, engineered R_g ~ 15 Ohm). What matters is whether
  gds*(Rg+Rs) and the Rg*Cgd feedback term are realistic, which is
  why this model now includes Rs (previously missing) and reports
  an explicit extrinsic (+pad capacitance) estimate alongside the
  intrinsic one, rather than asserting a required sign for f_max-f_T.
