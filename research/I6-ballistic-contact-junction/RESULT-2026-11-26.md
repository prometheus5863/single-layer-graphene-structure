# I6 — RESULT 2026-11-26: ballistic junction resistance on the gate-tuned Au edge (experiment 1)

**Script:** `run_ballistic_junction.py`. **Outputs:** `ballistic_junction_results.csv`, `ballistic_junction_transcript.txt`, `ballistic_junction.png`. src is imported, not edited.

## What was run
- **Profile.** I1's gate-tuned contact edge (11-23 and 11-24) was used unchanged: Au W − W0 = 0.59 eV, src C_I, gate charge included. One tail n(x) = n_b + (n_c − n_b)/(1 + x/λ) for λ = 5, 15, 27 (the Malec fit) and 250 nm (src). The graphene under the metal is the left lead at n_c. The tail is truncated at max(4 µm, 200λ), with a residual step of ≤ 5 % of n_b.
- **Ballistic model.** k_y-resolved massless Dirac equation at E_F with exact piecewise-constant slice propagators. These have no singularity at the p–n neutrality point. The transmitted state is propagated backwards with log-norm renormalisation; a first forward transfer-matrix version overflowed on the n-branch and was replaced before any number was used. Then G/W = (4e²/h)∫T dk_y/2π.
  - R_L is the total Landauer resistance.
  - R_J = R_L − R_Sh(n_b) is the junction excess over the channel's own Sharvin term.
- **Diffusive comparison.** src's R_extra on the same single tail, ∫[1/σ(n) − 1/σ(n_b)]dx with the puddle-regularised σ.
- **Densities.** |n| = 0.5–8 ×10¹² cm⁻² on both branches. The p-branch is p–p′ (Au p-dopes); the n-branch is a p–n junction.

## Validation (one, exact limit)
For a flat profile at 3×10¹² cm⁻², min T(k_y) = 1.000000000000 and R_L·W = 66.0373 Ω·µm, equal to the Sharvin value (h/4e²)π/k_F to a relative error of 1×10⁻¹³. **PASS.**

## What came out

|---|---|---|---|---|
| n-branch R_J / diffusive R_extra | 1.38–10.2 | 0.90–4.18 | 0.67–2.61 | 0.17–0.62 |
| ballistic ΔR = R(n) − R(p), Ω·µm | 214 → 135 | 349 → 148 | 445 → 162 | 1048 → 359 |

| p-branch: ballistic R_J / diffusive R_extra, Ω·µm | ≤ 0.06 / −107…−3 | ≤ 0.13 / −255…−8 | ≤ 0.17 / −428…−14 | ≤ 0.17 / −3966…−131 |

1. **The transport model matters, and in a way the diffusive model cannot reproduce.**
   - **p-branch (p–p′).** The ballistic junction adds essentially nothing (R_J < 0.2 Ω·µm), because the channel's Sharvin resistance is the bottleneck. src's diffusive R_extra is *negative* there, because the more heavily doped tail conducts better. A Landauer junction cannot give a negative excess.
   - **n-branch (p–n).** The ratio R_J/R_extra crosses 1 inside the window at every λ ≤ 27 nm. Ballistic is up to 10× larger at λ = 5 nm and high density, and the diffusive model is 1.6–6× larger at λ = 250 nm.
2. **The asymmetry sign matches Huard 2008 (ΔR > 0) on both models. The ballistic density dependence is much flatter:** slope −0.4 to −0.6 against diffusive −1.5. The ballistic slope is close to the Huard Eq. 3 sharp-junction slope (−0.5). At λ = 5–15 nm, ballistic ΔR has a minimum near 4–6 ×10¹² and then rises as the Au junction approaches closure (n_g* = 9.3×10¹²). This is closer to Huard's text ("R_odd saturates") than the diffusive 14× fall at λ = 27 nm, but it is not saturated: it falls 1.6× at λ = 5 nm and 2.7× at 27 nm. For λ ≤ 27 nm, ballistic ΔR sits 1.3–3.7× above the Huard Eq. 3 sharp-junction lower bound over the window, never below it, as it should for a smooth junction.
3. **Passi 2018 (Au, −40 V, 519 Ω·µm).** On the p-branch, neither junction model contributes: ballistic gives ≈ 0, diffusive gives −184…−30 Ω·µm. On this profile the measured Au R_c must be almost entirely metal-to-graphene injection (R_mg / transfer length). This strengthens the 11-24 I1 reading.

## Did the success metric move?
- **Kill condition: not hit.** R_J/R_extra leaves 0.5–2× at every λ.
- **Success metric, part 1 (> 2× difference at λ = 5–15 nm): met** on the n-branch at |n| ≳ 2.5×10¹² (5 nm) and ≳ 5×10¹² (15 nm). On the p-branch the two models differ in sign.
- **Part 2 (the ballistic model is closer to the measured Au R_c): not established.**
  - Passi's p-branch R_c is not a junction quantity in either model.
  - The Malec R_c peak position is fixed by junction closure in both models, by construction of the 11-23 fit.
  - Only the Huard asymmetry shape discriminates, and it favours ballistic (slope −0.4 to −0.6 vs −1.5, against Huard's "saturated"). That rests on text values only.

## Caveats
- Huard's contacts are Ti/Au, and only text constraints are available (the figures are inaccessible, see I1 11-25).
- The left lead is ideal graphene at n_c. The metal-injection term R_mg is not included, so R_L is a junction-plus-Sharvin quantity, not a full R_c.
- No disorder or puddles in the ballistic calculation; src's ℓ ≈ 66 nm at 2×10¹². The λ = 250 nm row is therefore outside the ballistic regime and is shown only as the src reference.
- The diffusive ΔR here is for a single tail, not the 11-25 TLM extraction, so its values differ from 11-25 (1091 vs 739 Ω·µm at 1.2×10¹², λ = 27 nm), but the slope is the same (−1.5 vs −1.4).
