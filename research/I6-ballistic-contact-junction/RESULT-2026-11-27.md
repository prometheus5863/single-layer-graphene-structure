# I6 — RESULT 2026-11-27: full ambipolar R_c(V_g) = R_mg + R_J with one parameter (experiment 2)

**Script:** `run_full_rc.py`. **Outputs:** `full_rc_results.csv`, `full_rc_transcript.txt`, `full_rc.png`. src is imported, not edited. The 11-26 functions are imported from `run_ballistic_junction.py`. Importing re-runs that script, and it rewrote its outputs byte-identically (git shows no diff).

## What was run
- **Metal injection R_mg.** Transmission-line model (Berger 1972): interface resistivity ρ_c plus the sheet resistance under the metal, R_s = 1/σ(n_c) (src's puddle-regularised σ, with n_c the gate-tuned Au edge density from I1). It is solved as a resistor ladder over the contact length L_c.
- **Full contact resistance.** R_c = R_mg(n_c) + R_J. R_J is the 11-26 ballistic Dirac transfer-matrix junction excess.
- **The one free parameter.** ρ_c was fixed so that R_c = 519 Ω·µm (Passi 2018, Au, −40 V; `../I1-tlm-on-doping-profile/passi2018_au_tlm.csv`). This was done at both ends of I1's density bracket, n_g = −2 and −5 ×10¹² cm⁻², and for L_c = 0.5, 1 and 10 µm.
- **Predictions, no refit:**
  - (a) Passi's Dirac-point R_c (1372 Ω·µm; ratio 2.64 to −40 V).
  - (b) Malec 2011: R_c(0 V)/R_c(−100 V) = 128/95 = 1.35, and the R_c maximum near +120 V. This uses Malec's C_g = 12.4 nF/cm², with Malec's 0 V assumed neutral as in I1.
  - At the CNP, both predictions average the n_g = ±n_puddle sides.
- **Prior-art read.** Xia et al., Nat. Nanotechnol. 6, 179 (2011), doi:[10.1038/nnano.2011.6](https://doi.org/10.1038/nnano.2011.6). Abstract only, via the [SUNY record](https://researchconnect.suny.edu/en/publications/the-origins-and-limits-of-metal-graphene-junction-resistance/); the full text was not fetched.

## Validation (one, exact limit)
The uniform ladder reproduces the closed form √(ρ_c R_s)·coth(L_c/L_T): 59.80465 vs 59.80465 Ω·µm, relative error 2×10⁻⁸. **PASS.**

## What came out
1. **Fitted ρ_c.** ρ_c = 2.4×10⁻⁶ to 1.5×10⁻⁵ Ω·cm² depending on L_c, with L_T = 1.0–2.9 µm.
   - At −40 V the junction term is negligible (R_J < 0.1 Ω·µm), so Passi's p-side R_c is entirely injection. This confirms the 11-26 reading.
   - L_T > L_c for L_c ≤ 1 µm, so the fit is in the short-contact regime, R_mg ≈ ρ_c/L_c. ρ_c is then degenerate with Passi's unknown contact length.
2. **Prediction (a), Passi Dirac point (λ = 27 nm).** The model gives R_c(CNP)/R_c(−40 V) = 1.80–2.09 against the measured 2.64, so it under-predicts by 21–32 %.
   - The two CNP sides differ strongly: about 530 Ω·µm on the p side (p–p′, no junction) and about 1350 Ω·µm on the n side (p–n).
   - The measured value sits close to the n side alone.
   - Not run: λ = 5 and 15 nm for this ratio. A smaller λ lowers R_J, so the shortfall would grow.
3. **Prediction (b), Malec.** R_c(0)/R_c(−100 V) = 1.51 (λ = 5 nm), 1.76 (15 nm) and 1.93 (27 nm), against the measured 1.35.
   - λ = 5 nm is closest, 12 % high. This is the same direction as I1's 11-21 finding that clean Au needs λ ≈ 5–15 nm.
   - The peak sits at +120 V, but that is by construction, because n_c → 0 there.
   - The peak is an unbroadened spike of about 9.9 kΩ·µm. Ballistic R_J diverges as the modes under the metal vanish, because there are no puddles in the ballistic leg. Its height has no meaning.
   - Absolute values are 4–5× Malec's, as expected with Passi's ρ_c on a different interface: Malec's Au grains were evaporated at 460 °C on baked graphene. Only the ratios are compared.
4. **Xia 2011 (abstract).** Pd only. R_c is attributed to transport in the graphene under the metal (coupling length vs mean free path), with about 75 % transfer efficiency and 110 ± 20 Ω·µm at 6 K. The abstract names no physisorbed metal and no p–n junction term. So the I6 kill clause "Xia/Cusati already give the per-metal physisorbed comparison" is **not hit on the abstract**. The full text, especially its gate-dependence model, still has to be checked before a novelty claim.

## Caveats
- ρ_c and L_c are degenerate (short-contact regime), and Passi's contact length is not in the repo's CSV.
- No puddle broadening in the ballistic R_J. This shapes the CNP and closure behaviour.
- Malec's V_g = 0 neutrality is an assumption (see the CSV note).
- Malec and Passi are text values only. No figure was digitised.
