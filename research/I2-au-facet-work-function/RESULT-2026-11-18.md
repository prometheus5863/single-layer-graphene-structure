# I2 — RESULT 2026-11-18: second DFT reference and one experimental Au sign

**Run:** `run_second_reference.py` imports `src/graphene_contact_doping_model.py` unchanged. It compares the model's contact Fermi shift at the facet-matched (111) work functions against three references:
- the **printed** ΔE_F of Khomyakov et al., PRB 79, 195425 (2009), Table I ([doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.79.195425](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.79.195425), [arXiv:0902.1203](https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.1203)), digitised to `khomyakov2009_table1.csv`;
- the W_covered − W_G proxy used on 2026-11-17;
- one experimental Au value: room-temperature STS of graphene on polycrystalline Au, with E_D 0.25–0.55 eV above E_F, which is p-type (Sławińska et al., PRB 85, 235430 (2012), [doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.85.235430](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.85.235430), [arXiv:1201.5243](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5243)), in `experimental_au_doping.csv`.

The run also feeds in the same table's **experimental** metal work functions, keeping W0 = 5.4 eV (a calculated value).

**Validation (exact limit):** ΔE_F,model(W0) = 0. It passes.

**What came out**
- Against the printed DFT Fermi shifts, the model gets **5/5 signs** with an **RMS error of 0.053 eV**. That is better than yesterday's 0.074 eV, which was measured against the proxy.
- The proxy itself is 0.089 eV RMS away from the printed values, so it was the weaker reference. W_cov − W_G is not exactly ΔE_F, because the interface dipole also shifts the covered work function. Yesterday's Cu "overshoot ×1.95" was mostly an artefact of the proxy: against the printed −0.17 eV, the model's −0.16 eV is within 0.02 eV.
- **Experiment:** STS on Au shows **p-type** doping, which matches the model's sign with calc (111) W. The model's magnitude, +0.13 eV, is below the measured 0.25–0.55 eV. Khomyakov's DFT value, +0.19 eV, is also below that range. The source paper attributes the larger shifts to surface disorder and amorphous Au regions, where its own DFT reaches +0.28 to +0.41 eV.
- **The sign depends on the source of W.** With the table's experimental W_M (Au 5.31 eV < 5.4 eV), the model predicts n-type Au, which contradicts both DFT and STS (4/5 signs, RMS 0.143 eV). W0 = 5.4 eV comes from the calculation, so W_M has to come from the same calculation. This confirms yesterday's conclusion with an independent check.

**Metric:** I2's kill condition, "no sign changes", is not hit. Au flips sign, and the experiment confirms the flip. The data comparison exists: 2 DFT tables and 1 experimental sign.

**Decision:** I2 is closed. It is a consistency fix with no standalone novelty, so it is **not** PAPER-READY. Its output is a validated doping input for the physisorbed metals: use calc (111) W with W0 = 5.4 eV, which gives Au as p-type at ≈ +0.13 to +0.19 eV. I1 (TLM) and I4 (normal-metal side of the junction) take that input. Whether to change §4.7 of the thesis is Harsh's decision.

**Caveats:** the STS sample is polycrystalline Au on a surface, not a lithographic contact edge. The footnote letters of the experimental W column in Khomyakov Table I were not read, and neither was the published PRB 85 version. The ΔE_F sign convention was inferred from the table signs and the paper's text.
