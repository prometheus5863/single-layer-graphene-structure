# I2 — RESULT 2026-11-17: facet-matched (111) work functions in the §4.7 contact model

**Run:** `run_facet_matched_W.py` (imports `src/graphene_contact_doping_model.py` unchanged). It feeds the DFT (111) work functions of Giovannetti et al., PRL 101, 026803 (2008) ([doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.101.026803](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.101.026803), [arXiv:0802.2267](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.2267)), Table I, into `contact_edge_carrier_density` and `junction_extra_resistance` at n_bulk = 2×10¹⁶ m⁻², the §4.7 on-state density. It then compares the contact Fermi-level shift with the DFT shift from the same table, digitised to `giovannetti2008_table1.csv` as ΔE_F = W_covered − W_G with W_G = 4.48 eV.

**Validation (exact limit):** at W = W0 = 5.4 eV the model gives n_contact = 0, so it passes.

**What came out**
- With facet-matched W, the model gets the doping sign right for **5/5** physisorbed metals against DFT. The RMS ΔE_F error is **0.074 eV**, smaller than the paper's own Eq. (1) at 0.112 eV. The largest misses are Cu, overshot ×1.95 (0.08 eV absolute), and Au, undershot ×0.50 (0.13 eV absolute).
- **Au's doping sign flips** from n (polycrystalline 5.10 eV, below W0) to p (5.54 eV), which agrees with DFT and with the paper's text. Au's contact then forms a p–n junction with the n-type channel, and R_extra goes from −161 to **+947 Ω·µm**.
- Cu's R_extra also changes sign (−445 → +33 Ω·µm), but the doping sign does not. At 5.22 eV, Cu n-dopes the contact more weakly than the gated bulk does (|ΔE_F| 0.16 eV against 0.165 eV for the bulk), so the near-contact region is now slightly *worse* than the bulk.
- Pt stays p-type. Its R_extra falls 6.6×, because the stronger p-doping narrows the low-density crossing.
- The 2026-11-16 corrected §4.7 claim was that "every physisorbed contact n-dopes and R_extra < 0". That claim depends on the work-function table. With W and W0 taken from the same DFT source, it holds only for Al and Ag. Cu, Au and Pt all give R_extra > 0.

**Metric:** I2's kill condition was "no sign changes for any physisorbed metal". It is **not hit**, because Au flips. I2 has no separate success metric. The comparison against reference data now exists (figure `facet_matched_W.png`, left panel).

**Caveats:** the reference values are DFT, not experiment. ΔE_F is derived from Table I's W column, not read from a printed ΔE_F, and Figs. 3 and 5 of the paper were not digitised. W0 = 5.4 eV in `src/` is itself taken from this paper, so the sign test is not independent of the source. The ΔE_F magnitudes are independent, because they come from the repo's C_interface/C_q model.

**Novelty:** none by itself. This is an internal-consistency fix to §4.7 that Giovannetti 2008 and Khomyakov 2009 already imply. Whether to change the thesis text is Harsh's decision. It is useful as the doping input for I1 (TLM) and I4 (normal-metal side of the junction).
