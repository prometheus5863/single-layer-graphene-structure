# I1 — RESULT 2026-11-21: clean Au contacts (Malec & Davidović 2011) — sign right, tail length wrong

**Source:** C. E. Malec and D. Davidović, "Electronic properties of clean Au-graphene contacts", Phys. Rev. B 84, 033407 (2011), doi:[10.1103/PhysRevB.84.033407](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.84.033407), arXiv:[1108.3238](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.3238). This is the dataset named as the next experiment on 11-20. The authors evaporated Au at about 460 °C after a 12 h vacuum bake at 250 °C. The Au forms isolated grains directly on exfoliated SLG (300 nm SiO₂), with no resist and no adhesion layer under the grains. The values come from the arXiv text only, because the figures are images: see `malec2011_clean_au.csv`. Text values used:
- p-doping.
- Bulk four-probe R maximum at V_g = +85 V (high coverage, 4.2 K).
- Contact-resistance maximum near +120 V, which equals 9.3×10¹² cm⁻² of holes under the grains, or ΔE_F = 0.35 eV.
- C_g = 12.4 nF/cm².
- Typical graphene gap between grains: L = 35 nm.

**Caveat:** the text does not give the control (no-grain) sample's peak position, so shifts are measured from V_g = 0.

**Run:** `run_malec_clean_au.py`, with src unchanged. It reuses the 11-20 gap construction (bounded S_or, pinned n_c, src puddle-regularised σ), with λ as a parameter and Malec's C_g. Output files: `malec_clean_au_transcript.txt`, `malec_clean_au_results.csv` and `malec_clean_au.png`.

**Validation (exact limit):** with n_c = 0, V_NP(gap) = 0 for λ = 5, 50 and 250 nm (|V| ≤ 2×10⁻⁶ V). It passes.

**What came out**

| test | model (src, Au calc (111) W = 5.54 eV, λ = 250 nm) | Malec 2011 (clean Au) | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| doping sign | p (n_c = −1.25×10¹² cm⁻²) | p | **match** |
| ΔE_F under contact | +0.13 eV | +0.35 eV (DFT, Khomyakov 2009: +0.19) | 2.7× low in E_F, 7.4× low in n; needs src-equivalent W = 6.08 eV |
| neutrality of a 35 nm gap | +29 000 V (with Malec's n_c) / +4 400 V (with the model's n_c) | +85 V | **fails by ×340 / ×51** |
| tail length needed | — | λ ≈ 5 nm (puts the gap V_NP at +85 V with Malec's n_c); λ ≈ 15 nm (matches the gap/contact density ratio 85/120 = 0.71 at mid-gap) | src's 250 nm is 17–50× too long |

1. **Sign:** with clean, adhesion-free Au, the src W → n_c rule gives the right sign (p-type). This supports the 11-20 reading that Nouchi's n-type Au comes from the Cr underlayer. Clean-contact Au experiments now agree with the model's sign 2/2: STS (Sławińska 2012) and transport (Malec 2011).
2. **Magnitude:** the model's contact density is about 7× too small. Malec's own theory comparison (0.35 eV at a ~4 Å separation) suggests a larger d_eq than the 3.3–3.5 Å src assumes.
3. **Tail length (the main result):** with λ = 250 nm the doping tail pins the whole 35 nm gap, and the gate cannot reach neutrality below thousands of volts. The measured gaps go through neutrality at +85 V, which needs λ ≈ 5–15 nm. The data also contradict the pinned-n_c assumption itself: the contact-resistance maximum at +120 V means that even graphene under the Au is gate-tuned.
4. **Tension with 11-20:** with λ = 5 nm, Nouchi's −28 V shift needs n_c = +4.4×10¹³ cm⁻² (4.7× Malec's density, and the wrong sign for Au). With λ = 250 nm it needs only +4.5×10¹¹. **No single (W-rule, λ) in the current src profile fits both datasets.** The f = 1/(1+x/λ) profile with a gate-independent pinned edge is the weak point, and the W → sign rule is not.

**Metric:** I1 now has two pass/fail data comparisons. The sign passes on clean contacts. The tail length fails by more than an order of magnitude on clean contacts. The I1 kill condition (TLM absorbs < 10 % of R_extra) is not hit. With λ ≈ 5–15 nm, though, the 11-19 R_extra and TLM window-dependence numbers (computed at 250 nm) would shrink strongly. That has not been rerun. I1 is not PAPER-READY.
