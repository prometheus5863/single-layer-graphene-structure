# I1 — RESULT 2026-11-19b: first comparison with published Au TLM numbers (Harsh asked to continue the run)

**Source:** Passi et al., arXiv:1807.04772 / Adv. Mater. Interfaces (2018), [doi:10.1002/admi.201801285](https://doi.org/10.1002/admi.201801285). This is the same paper `src/` uses for the Au R_c.

I read the v1 PDF text. The figure images were not available, and neither was Supplementary Fig. S3, which holds the other TLM structures. As a result **no R_tot(L) points could be digitised**. arxiv.org was also blocked from both sandboxes. The numbers that could be used are in `passi2018_au_tlm.csv`:
- R_c is 1372 Ω·µm at the Dirac point (V_bg ≈ 38 V) and 519 Ω·µm at V_bg = −40 V, which is a hole-doped channel.
- The paper reports R² > 0.984 for all TLM fits.
- Its DFT surface doping is E_D − E_F = +0.14 eV (p-type). The model with I2's calc (111) W gives +0.13 eV.

**Run:** `run_passi_comparison.py` reuses the I1 simulation, with src unchanged. The paper gives no channel densities. I therefore bracketed the −40 V channel with p-type n_bulk = −2×10¹⁶ and −5×10¹⁶ m⁻², used ≈ 0 for the Dirac point, and used +2×10¹⁶ as the I1 baseline. Three fit windows were tried: 1–10, 2–20 and 0.5–5 µm. Re-importing the I1 script left its outputs byte-identical.

**Validation (exact limit):** with n_c = n_bulk, R² = 1.000000000000. It passes.

**What came out**
1. **Passi's data does not contradict the model.** In the hole-doped channel the Au contact and the channel are both p-type, so there is no p–n junction and R(L) stays linear (R² ≥ 0.988). That matches the reported R² > 0.984. The junction term accounts for **24–55 %** of the measured 519 Ω·µm, which puts the interface/transmission part at about 230–400 Ω·µm.
2. **The curvature prediction cannot be tested on this paper.** It applies only to an n-type channel under p-doping Au (or a p-type channel under Al/Ag), where R² drops to 0.91–0.98. Passi measured only at the Dirac point and at −40 V (p), so I1's distinguishing prediction is untested so far.
3. **At the Dirac point the model's junction term is negative** (−690 to −1380 Ω·µm), because the doped contact region conducts better than the neutral channel. Matching Passi's 1372 Ω·µm would then need R_int ≈ 2.1–2.8 kΩ·µm. That is much larger than the 230–400 Ω·µm implied at −40 V. The puddle-regularised Drude σ near neutrality is the weakest part of the src model, so treat this as a flag and do not read it as a finding.

**Metric:** I1 now has its first data comparison, but it is a weak one: one consistency check, not a test the model could have failed. The kill condition is still not hit, and I1 is not PAPER-READY.
