# I1 — RESULT 2026-11-23: gate-tuned contact edge fixes the clean-Au tail length; Nouchi magnitude off by 1.8× out of sample

**Run:** `run_gate_tuned_edge.py`, with src unchanged. Output files: `gate_tuned_edge_transcript.txt`, `gate_tuned_edge_results.csv` and `gate_tuned_edge.png`. Data are the text values already in `malec2011_clean_au.csv` (Malec & Davidović, PRB 84, 033407 (2011), arXiv:[1108.3238](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.3238)) and `nouchi2011_vnp_vs_L.csv` (Nouchi et al., arXiv:[1102.2954](https://arxiv.org/abs/1102.2954)).

**What changed:** only the contact edge. src models the contact as graphene's quantum capacitance C_q in series with C_I = ε0/3.5 Å, with the edge density pinned. Here the back-gate charge n_g = C_g V_g/e enters the same electrostatics:

  E_F(n)/e + e(n − n_g)/C_I = −(W_M − W0)/e

So the graphene under the metal is gate-tuned with leverage C_q/(C_q + C_I). That leverage is 0 at neutrality and grows with |n|. The contact is neutral exactly at n_g* = C_I (W_M − W0)/e². The tail keeps f = 1/(1+x/λ) with the bounded two-contact S_or from 11-20, and λ is free. Khomyakov 2010's gate screening of the tail is **not** implemented yet.

**Validation (exact limit):** with C_I → 10⁻¹² F/m² (metal decoupled), n_c = n_g to within 5×10² cm⁻² over n_g = −5×10¹² to +7×10¹² cm⁻². That is the analytic O(C_I) residual, so it passes. (The first run reported FAIL because of a metric bug: it divided by 1 m⁻² at n_g = 0. It was fixed to an absolute tolerance before any result was read.)

1. **Gate-tuned edge removes the 11-21 contradiction.** Once the graphene under Au follows the gate, a 35 nm gap passes through neutrality at a sensible voltage for any λ. The +85 V point fixes λ ≈ 27 nm. That is longer than 11-21's 5–15 nm, which the pinned edge biased short. λ = 250 nm (src) overshoots by only 31 V instead of a factor of 340.
2. **Reinterpreting Malec's contact density.** Malec read +120 V as 9.3×10¹² cm⁻² (0.35 eV), assuming the gate fully reaches the graphene under Au. With C_q/C_I sharing, the same +120 V gives 5.1×10¹² cm⁻² (0.26 eV). That is closer to the DFT +0.19 eV (Khomyakov 2009) quoted by Malec. The src Au(111) value is still 2× low in E_F, and it puts the R_c maximum at +29 V.
3. **Side finding (src, not edited):** src's linearised `contact_edge_carrier_density` gives −1.25×10¹² cm⁻² for Au(111). The exact series equation it approximates gives −0.69×10¹² cm⁻², so the linearisation overestimates |n_c| by 1.8×. Promoting this to src is Harsh's call.
4. **One parameter set across both datasets:** Malec has 2 numbers and the model has 2 parameters, so Malec alone is not a test. Nouchi is the only out-of-sample check. On magnitude the model gives −15.6 V against −28 V measured (0.56×), compared with a 4.7× mismatch in n_c on 11-21. The sign is still opposite: Nouchi's Au has a Cr adhesion layer and is n-type, so only the magnitude is compared. In the third panel of the figure the curve turns back above |W − W0| ≈ 7 eV. That is the ±300 V search window clipping, not physics.

**Metric:** I1's model now passes the clean-Au tail-length test it failed on 11-21. It predicts an out-of-sample magnitude within 2× and has one sign mismatch that is attributed to a different contact stack.
